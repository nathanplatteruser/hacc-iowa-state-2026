Iron Man, Not Autopilot

Level-4 gates: when an AI system should stop and hand the work back to a human. Nathan Platter

Synthetic data. Every company, person, and number in the demo and examples is invented for teaching. No product pitch.

The rule

"My agent [does the job], and it stops and hands the work to [a named person] before it [changes access, moves money, or touches production]."

Five signals that mean stop

  1. Untrusted input carries instructions (prompt injection, OWASP LLM01:2025).
  2. It holds more permission than the job needs (excessive agency, OWASP LLM06:2025).
  3. The action cannot be undone.
  4. It cannot verify the claim.
  5. It is out of its lane.

A Level-4 gate

  • Machine checks that fail closed. Default deny. Errors mean no.
  • A human who signs off. Named role, separate identity from the agent.
  • An audit trail. Every allow, block, and decision, where the agent cannot edit it.
